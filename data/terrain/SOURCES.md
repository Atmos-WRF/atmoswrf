# Terrain

`gmted2010_us_30s.npz.xz` (an xz-compressed npz): ground height in metres above mean sea level (int16) over the lower 48
states and their borders (20–55° N, 130–60° W, 4200 × 8400 cells, row 0 = south; `lat0`/`lon0` = the south-west cell
centre, `d` = 1/120°), cut from WRF's static geography `topo_gmted2010_30s` (WPS_GEOG, as distributed by NCAR/UCAR for
WRF; here from ArWen Desktop's copy). GMTED2010 (Global Multi-resolution Terrain Elevation Data 2010) is by the U.S.
Geological Survey and the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, public domain.
